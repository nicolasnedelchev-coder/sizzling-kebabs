Welcome to Sizzling Kebabs

Persian Flavours, Cooked Over Charcoal

Our kebabs are grilled over charcoal in the traditional Persian way, with fluffy rice, fresh salads and warm naan. Come in for a classic koobideh, a sharing platter, or something from our wider menu of burgers, seafood and doner.

Whether you're here for a quick bite or a family meal, we want every visit to feel warm, generous and full of flavour.
